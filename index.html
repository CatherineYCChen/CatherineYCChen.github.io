Research

My research focuses on statistical decision-making and optimization under uncertainty, with an emphasis on risk-sensitive and sequential methods. I develop theoretically grounded algorithms for controlling tail risk and making reliable decisions under distributional uncertainty, with applications including but not limited to, AI, finance, and economics.
